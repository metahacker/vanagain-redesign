Navigation design

Two questions.
No menus.

Everyone who lands on a parts store is answering the same two questions: what van and what part of it. The navigation is just those two answers, always on screen. Nothing hides behind a hover.

Axis 1 · set once, remembered

Seven fitment groups, taken straight from the store's own product tags. Set it once and every count, list and part page filters to it.

Under the hood

URLs that say what they are

Van and system live in the path, so every combination is a real, linkable, indexable page. Filters stay in the query string.

/shop
/shop/vanagon-2-1l ← 1,294 parts
/shop/vanagon-2-1l/brakes ← 104 parts
/shop/vanagon-2-1l/brakes?condition=used&sort=price
/shop/bus/exhaust ← 40 parts
/garage ← the van picker
Nothing on hover

Every choice is visible or one tap away on its own page. Works the same with a mouse, a thumb or a screen reader.

Fitment out of the title

"Brake caliper carrier" plus a fitment line, instead of "Brake Caliper Carrier: Vanagon 1986-1991". Titles get short; fit becomes filterable.

Counts everywhere

Every van and system shows how many parts it holds. Owners see right away that the store is deep for their van.
