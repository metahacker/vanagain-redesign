Pick once. The whole store filters to it, and every part page says plainly whether it fits.

Air-cooled or water-cooled?

Water-cooled Vanagons (mid-1983 on) have a second grille under the headlights for the front radiator. Air-cooled vans have one.

1.9L or 2.1L?

The engine code is stamped on the case. DH is the 1.9L, MV is the 2.1L. Syncro 4WD vans use the 2.1L list.
